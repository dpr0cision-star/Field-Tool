Codes default to the ANSI/SDI A250.14 prep names - replace them with your Curries / EDGE codes. Saved on this device for every project; the export uses them for list, discount and net.
CURRIES 2026 PRICE BOOK LOADED
Frame, door, extras, lite kit, louver and glass list prices are built in from NESC Curries Pricing 2026 (price date 4/1/2026). Net = list x multiplier. Doors: 0.38 (6 or fewer) / 0.35 (7-11) / 0.32 (12+). Frames: Standard 0.41 / 0.39 / 0.38 by quantity, 10-Day 0.44, 5-Day 0.55. Welding 0.34.
